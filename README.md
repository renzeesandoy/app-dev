Overview

Spider-Man: No Way Home follows Peter Parker after his identity as Spider-Man is exposed to the world. Seeking help from Doctor Strange, Peter accidentally opens the multiverse, bringing villains and alternate Spider-Men from other universes into his own world.


Main Characters

1. Peter Parker / Spider-Man – A young superhero trying to balance his life and responsibilities.
2. Doctor Strange – The Master of the Mystic Arts who helps Peter fix his reality.
3. MJ (Michelle Jones) – Peter’s girlfriend and emotional support.
4. Ned Leeds – Peter’s best friend and loyal ally.
5. Green Goblin (Norman Osborn) – A dangerous villain from another universe.
6. Doc Ock (Otto Octavius) – A brilliant scientist with mechanical tentacles.
7. Electro (Max Dillon) – A powerful being made of pure electricity.
